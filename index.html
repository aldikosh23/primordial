    Creatures never eat others of a similar colour. So prey evolve to look like their hunters, and hunters evolve away — the whole ecosystem slowly spins around the colour wheel. Biologists call this kind of endless arms race the Red Queen effect.

    World rules

    How it works
    • Genes. Speed, size, vision, diet and colour. Children inherit them with small random mutations.
    • Energy. Everything costs energy: being big, being fast, seeing far. Run out and you starve.
    • Diet. Plant-eaters graze, meat-eaters hunt anything no bigger than themselves. Omnivores do a bit of both, worse.
    • Cover. Fertile zones are thick with plants. Small creatures inside them are hard to spot.
    • Selection. Nothing is scripted. Whoever eats well has more children, so their genes spread.
